# Carlspat Private School Management System — User Manual

*Emphasis on All-Round Development*

Everyone signs in at the same address with their email and password. The system
shows each person only what their role allows. It works on phones, tablets and
computers, with light and dark mode (sun/moon button, top right).

---

## 1. School Admin

### First-day setup (Settings → School Settings)
1. **School Information** — edit the school name, motto, address, phone, email,
   head teacher's name, and upload the school logo. These appear instantly on
   the login page, portal, receipts and report cards. *No programmer needed.*
2. **Sessions & Terms** — create the academic session (e.g. `2026/2027`) and its
   three terms; click **Make current** on the active term. Everything (fees,
   scores, attendance, reports) is scoped to the current term.
3. **Grading System** — edit the assessment components (First Test, Second Test,
   Assignment, Project, Mid-Term Exam, Final Exam) and their max scores (must
   total 100), and the grade bands (A 70–100 Excellent, etc.).

### Daily work
- **Students** → *Register Student*: bio-data, passport photo, class, parent,
  medical info (blood group, genotype, allergies), previous school. The
  admission number (CPS/2026/0001) is generated automatically.
- **Teachers** → *Add Teacher*: creates their login; assign subjects per class
  under Classes.
- **User Accounts**: create bursar/parent/student logins, disable accounts,
  reset passwords.
- **Fees** → *Set Fee*: amount per class per category (Tuition, PTA, Examination,
  Development Levy, Transport…). The **Debtors** panel lists every owing student
  with parent phone numbers.
- **Announcements**: publish to everyone or just teachers/parents/students, with
  optional email and SMS broadcast.
- **End of session** → Students → select pupils → *Promote* to the next class, or
  graduate the finalists (kept in records with full history).

## 2. Teachers

- **Attendance**: pick your class and date, tap **Present / Late / Absent** per
  pupil (or *All present*), then **Save Register**. One register per day —
  re-saving corrects it.
- **Results**: pick class + subject; type scores into the sheet (the system
  blocks scores above each component's maximum); press **Save** above each
  column. You can only enter scores for subjects assigned to you.
- **Report card comments**: via Results → comments (form teachers).
- **Messages**: write to parents and other staff.

## 3. Parents

- **Dashboard** shows each child with their fee status at a glance.
- **Fees**: see the exact breakdown (tuition, PTA, …), what you've paid, and the
  balance. **Pay online** with your card via Paystack, or pay cash/transfer at
  the bursar's office — either way a receipt appears under **Payments**.
- **Report Cards**: choose the child and term.
  - ✅ **Fees fully paid** → download/print the official PDF report card.
  - 🔒 **Any balance outstanding** → you will see:
    *“Access to report card is restricted until school fees have been fully paid.”*
    Pay the balance and the download unlocks immediately.
- **Messages / Announcements**: school news and direct contact with teachers.

## 4. Students

View your own profile, attendance record, subject-by-subject results,
announcements, and (when fees are settled) your report card.

## 5. Bursar / Accountant

- **Payments** → *Record Payment*: select the pupil, amount and method (cash,
  bank transfer, card, POS, cheque). A numbered PDF receipt
  (CPS-RCP-2026-00001) is issued instantly and the parent gets an email.
- **Fees**: maintain the fee structure; monitor the debtors list and total
  outstanding.
- Online (Paystack/Flutterwave) payments confirm automatically — never mark
  them manually.

## 6. Super Admin

Everything the school admin can do, plus creating admin accounts and reviewing
the **audit log** (every login, edit, payment and download, with time and IP).

---

## The report card

The PDF report card contains: school logo and contact details, the pupil's
passport photo, class, position in class (e.g. *3rd of 25*), each subject's
First Test / Second Test / Assignment / Project / Mid-Term / Exam scores, total,
percentage and grade, attendance summary, class teacher's and head teacher's
comments, a space for the school stamp, and a **QR code** — anyone (e.g. a new
school) can scan it to confirm the report is genuine.

## Frequently asked questions

**I forgot my password.** Ask the school admin to reset it (User Accounts).
**The report card button is grey.** There is an outstanding fee balance — check the Fees page.
**A score was entered wrongly.** The teacher re-enters it and saves; the sheet updates in place.
**Attendance was marked for the wrong day.** Re-open that date and correct it.
